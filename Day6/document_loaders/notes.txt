Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding language, recognizing images, and making decisions. AI is widely used in various fields such as healthcare, education, finance, transportation, and entertainment. Virtual assistants, recommendation systems, self-driving cars, and chatbots are common examples of AI applications. With advancements in machine learning and deep learning, AI continues to improve rapidly. Although AI offers many benefits by increasing efficiency and automation, it also raises concerns related to privacy, ethics, and job displacement, making responsible development important.
